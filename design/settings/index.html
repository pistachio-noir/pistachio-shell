Pistachio Noir · Design proposal · 10 November 2026

Desktop settings, in their own window.

A normal, movable settings window with a sidebar and room for devices, sound and shell preferences. The launcher keeps the controls you reach for in a hurry.

Start with AppearanceSee SoundSee LensWhat stays in the launcher

In the settings window

Appearance, brightness and night light, sound and app volumes, notification schedules and rules, Bluetooth pairing, wired network status, LocalSend preferences, Lens, apps and places, system readings and About.

Still close at hand

The launcher keeps volume, brightness, Bluetooth, Night light and Do not disturb, plus an entry into Settings. Its existing app/window/workspace modes and power actions stay available. Quick settings continues to offer everyday controls.

Static review boards. Sidebar links, tabs and selected menus connect the illustrated states; controls do not change your system. All names, devices, locations and readings are made up. Approval is for the design first.

Proposed window: 1040 × 760 on a 1280 × 900 board. Native window focus, movement and resizing are implementation work after approval. Changes apply immediately; text edits use Save / Cancel. No shell code has been changed for this proposal.
